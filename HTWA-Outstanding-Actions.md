- Driver counts as a cost-share (÷ seats +1) — load-bearing for the licensing position.
- Booking fee display: one headline total + tappable breakdown ("Service charge" 10% + "Booking fee" flat £2/€2).
- Mileage rates: admin-editable config table, official HMRC + Revenue figures, keyed by driver tax residence (not journey origin).
- UK: miles / GBP / 55p first 10k miles then 25p. ROI: km / EUR / banded by engine cc, floor to whole cent.
- UK rounding: whole pence, no special rule needed.
- Cumulative annual mileage total reset: UK 6 April, ROI 1 January.
