# htwa Cookie Policy

**Effective date:** 1 June 2026

htwa uses minimal cookies and local storage. At launch, we do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

## What we use

| Type | Purpose | Duration |
|------|---------|---------|
| Authentication session token | Keeps you logged in (Supabase auth) | Session / 7 days |
| AsyncStorage (native) | Caches your nominated contact details for offline access | Until app uninstall |

## What we do NOT use

- Google Analytics or similar tracking
- Advertising or retargeting pixels
- Third-party marketing cookies

## Changes

If we introduce analytics in future, we will update this policy and request your consent beforehand.

Contact privacy@htwa-app.com with questions.
