# HTWA Community Safety Pledge

*Every HTWA user agrees to this pledge before accessing the platform.*

---

## Our Promise to Each Other

HTWA works because people trust each other. That trust is built on a simple idea: we're all just heading that way anyway, and we've agreed to share the journey properly.

When you join HTWA, you're joining a community. This pledge is what that community stands for.

---

## I Pledge To:

**Be who I say I am.**  
I've completed ID verification honestly. My profile is accurate. I won't share my account with anyone else.

**Travel safely.**  
If I'm a driver, I'll drive to the standard I'd want if I were the passenger. I'll follow the rules of the road, arrive on time, and make sure my car is safe and insured. If I'm a passenger, I'll be where I said I'd be, when I said I'd be there.

**Be fair with money.**  
I understand that HTWA is a cost-sharing platform, not a taxi service. If I'm a driver, I'll only charge my share of the journey cost — never more. I'll always pay and collect through the app.

**Treat people with respect.**  
I'll be kind, considerate and decent to every person I share a journey with — regardless of their background, gender, nationality or any other characteristic. There's no room for harassment, discrimination or abuse on HTWA.

**Use the safety features.**  
I'll set up a nominated contact. I'll share my journey when it feels right. I know the Silent SOS button is there if I ever need it — and I hope I never do.

**Speak up.**  
If something makes me uncomfortable, I'll report it. If I see something that doesn't seem right, I'll flag it to the HTWA team at hello@htwa-app.com. A safe platform depends on all of us.

**Keep personal details private.**  
I won't share other users' personal information outside the app. What happens on a journey stays between the people on it — unless there's a safety concern.

---

## What HTWA Pledges to You:

**We verify everyone.**  
We require ID and selfie verification for all users prior to use, subject to technical rollout and legal requirements. A verified badge indicates a user has completed this process.

**We enforce the cost cap.**  
We enforce fare caps and will block charges that exceed the calculated journey cost. In exceptional technical or operational circumstances, manual review may apply.

**We take safety seriously.**  
We provide safety features including live tracking, nominated contacts, and Silent SOS. While these features are designed to enhance safety, they do not guarantee safety in all circumstances and are subject to availability at rollout.

**We listen.**  
If something goes wrong, contact us at hello@htwa-app.com. We'll take it seriously and act quickly.

**We're building this for Ireland.**  
HTWA exists because Irish students and commuters deserve a better, safer, more affordable way to get around. Everything we do is in service of that.
