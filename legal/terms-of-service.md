## 1. What htwa is

htwa is a **cost-sharing carpool platform**, not a taxi or private hire service. Drivers share the actual cost of their journey with passengers. Drivers may never profit from a ride — this is the legal and contractual basis on which htwa operates.

## 3. Driver obligations

By posting a ride as a driver you agree that:

3.1 You hold a full, valid driving licence for the vehicle you are driving.

3.2 Your vehicle is roadworthy, taxed, and insured. You are responsible for ensuring your insurance covers use as a carpool (cost-sharing) vehicle. Most standard comprehensive policies cover cost-sharing; check with your insurer.

3.3 The price per seat you set does not exceed the journey cost calculated using civil service mileage rates (Revenue.ie rates for ROI-based drivers; HMRC AMAP rates for NI-based drivers). htwa enforces this cap automatically.

3.4 You will not charge passengers more than the amount displayed at booking, regardless of changes to the journey.

3.5 You will not request cash payments outside the app.

3.6 You will complete the journey as described or notify passengers promptly if you need to cancel.

4.1 You will pay the agreed amount through the app before or at the time of the journey.

4.2 You will behave respectfully toward the driver and other passengers.

4.3 You will not request the driver to make unplanned stops or route deviations not agreed in advance.

## 5. The legal cost-share model

htwa operates under the carpooling exemption in Irish and UK transport law. The platform enforces:

- **Maximum price cap:** the total amount charged to all passengers cannot exceed the total journey cost under civil service mileage rates
- **No profit:** drivers may not earn more than their costs
- This is what distinguishes htwa from a taxi or SPSV service

Any attempt to circumvent this model (charging cash, manipulating route distances, creating fictitious bookings) is a breach of these Terms and may result in account termination and referral to relevant authorities.
