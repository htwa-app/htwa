How it works

Three steps, no faff.

1

Verify your identity

Upload a government ID and take a quick selfie. Every HTWA user is verified before they can book or offer a ride — no anonymity.

2

Find or offer a ride

Search for available journeys or post your own route. Prices are calculated automatically based on government mileage rates — drivers share costs, never profit.

3

Travel safely together

Live location is shared with your nominated contact the moment your journey starts. In-app messaging, a silent SOS button, and a women-only mode are all built in.
