## Full Description (4000 chars max)

**Save money on every journey. Travel safer together.**

htwa is Ireland's first cost-sharing rideshare app — built for students and commuters travelling between Irish cities, universities, and across the ROI/NI border.

**How it works**
Drivers share the real cost of their journey with passengers. The price is calculated from civil service mileage rates (Revenue.ie for ROI; HMRC AMAP for NI) and the cap is enforced automatically. No profit. Completely legal.

**For passengers** — Search rides, book a seat, pay securely in-app. See your savings vs bus and train on every trip.

**For drivers** — Post a ride in 60 seconds. Set your route, seats, and time — htwa does the cost calculation. Get paid to your bank via Stripe.

**Safety features**
✓ Government ID + selfie verification on every account  
✓ Women-only mode (driver and passenger)  
✓ Live tracking link sent to your nominated contact  
✓ Silent SOS alert with live location  

**Ireland-first design**
Built for Irish routes, Irish prices, and Irish students. Euro and Sterling. ROI + NI cross-border corridor supported.
