## Description (4000 chars max)

**Save money on every journey. Travel safer together.**

htwa is Ireland's first cost-sharing rideshare app — built specifically for students and commuters travelling between Irish cities, universities, and across the border.

**How it works**
Drivers share the real cost of their journey with passengers — no profit, no licensing, no grey area. htwa calculates the legal maximum using Irish and UK civil service mileage rates and enforces the cap automatically.

**For passengers**
Search for rides, book a seat, and pay securely in-app. See exactly what you're saving vs the bus or train on every trip.

**For drivers**
Post a ride in under 60 seconds. Set your route, departure time, and available seats — htwa calculates a fair price split for you. Get paid directly to your bank via Stripe.

**Safety first**
Every account is identity-verified before they can book or post a ride. Our safety features include:

- 🛡️ **Verified IDs** — government ID + selfie verification on every account
- 👩 **Women-only mode** — female drivers can restrict their ride to female passengers only; female passengers can filter to women-only rides
- 📍 **Nominated contact** — your trusted contact receives a live tracking link every time you travel (no app required for them)
- 🆘 **Silent SOS** — one tap sends a silent alert with your live location to your nominated contact

**Ireland-first**
htwa was built for Irish routes, Irish prices, and Irish students. Euro and Sterling supported. Cross-border corridor (ROI ↔ Northern Ireland) fully supported.
