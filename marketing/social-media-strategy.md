# HTWA — Social Media & Waiting List Strategy
> Summer 2026 — Building to critical mass before September launch

---

## The Goal

Arrive at September launch with enough waiting list signups across target universities that the app has rides to show on day one. A rideshare app with no rides is useless — critical mass is everything.

**Target:** 500+ verified waiting list signups before launch, spread across at least 6 universities.

**Priority universities (Phase 1):**
- UCD (Dublin)
- UCC (Cork)
- NUIG / University of Galway
- TCD (Dublin)
- QUB (Belfast)
- Ulster University (Derry/Jordanstown)
- DCU (Dublin)
- Maynooth University

---

## Platform Focus

**Instagram (@htwa.app) — primary platform.**  
University students in Ireland are on Instagram. It's where you build community, not just followers. Every post should feel like it was made by someone who actually uses the routes.

**TikTok — secondary (if time allows).**  
Short-form video of real routes, real prices, relatable student moments. High organic reach potential.

**No Twitter/X. No LinkedIn. No Facebook (unless targeting mature students or parents).**

---

## Brand Voice

- **Warm and human.** Never corporate. Never startup-speak.
- **Irish.** Use Irish place names, routes, university slang. "Heading to Galway for the weekend?" not "Commuting to your destination."
- **Honest about the early days.** People respect founders who are real about building something. Don't pretend to be bigger than you are.
- **Safety-first without being preachy.** The safety features sell themselves — show them, don't lecture.
- **Female-coded without excluding men.** The brand should feel safe for women without making male users feel unwelcome. Tone is inclusive, not exclusive.

## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1 — "The Route" (40% of posts)
Real Irish routes with real prices. Make people think "I do that journey every week."

Examples:
- "Dublin to Galway. Bus: €15. Train: €32. HTWA: estimated €7. 🟢" [map graphic with route]
- "The Belfast–Dublin corridor. One of the busiest student routes in Ireland. Coming September." [stylised route chip design]
- "Cork to Limerick on a Friday evening. Sound like you? Join the list." [relatable copy + QR code]

### Pillar 2 — "Built With You In Mind" (25% of posts)
Safety features, verification, women-only mode. Show the product without being a product demo.

Examples:
- "Every driver and passenger on HTWA is ID verified before they can use the app. That green tick means something." [close-up of verified badge UI]
- "Women-only journeys available. Because sometimes you just want that." [simple, no fuss]
- "Your nominated contact gets a live tracking link the moment your journey starts. Like having someone riding with you, without the awkward silence." [journey share UI screenshot]

### Pillar 3 — "Real People, Real Journeys" (20% of posts)
UGC, testimonials, behind-the-scenes of building. Authenticity beats polish.

Examples:
- Founder story posts — Jordan talking about why he built this
- "We're building HTWA this summer. Here's what we got done this week." [build progress]
- Student testimonials once beta testers are on board
- "Meet the routes we're launching with in September." [university-specific posts]

### Pillar 4 — "Join Us" (15% of posts)
Direct calls to action. Waiting list, QR code, website.

Examples:
- "September launch. Limited spots. Scan to join the list." [QR code post]
- "We're launching at [University] in September. Are you in?" [university-tagged post]
- Countdown posts as September approaches

## Posting Schedule

**Frequency:** 4–5 posts per week on Instagram. Consistency beats volume.

**Best times (Irish student audience):**
- Tuesday–Thursday: 6pm–9pm
- Sunday: 7pm–10pm
- Avoid Monday mornings and Friday afternoons

**Weekly rhythm:**
- Monday: Route post (savings comparison)
- Wednesday: Safety/product feature
- Friday: Relatable student content or founder update
- Sunday: Join the waiting list CTA

---

## Physical Marketing (High Priority)

### Flyers
- A5, already designed
- Target: student union noticeboards, library entrances, café bulletin boards, accommodation blocks
- **Best timing:** First week back in September (freshers week) — but also do a pre-launch drop in May/June while students are still on campus for exams

### Branded Coasters
- QR code + "Heading That Way Anyway?" + htwa-app.com
- Place in student union bars, campus cafés, library coffee areas
- These stay for weeks — high passive exposure

### Where to distribute:
- Student union buildings (ask permission — most are happy with local startups)
- Campus libraries (noticeboards near entrance)
- Campus cafés and common rooms
- Student accommodation common rooms
- Freshers fair stalls (book a table — usually cheap or free for startups)

## Paid Social (When Budget Allows)

**Platform:** Instagram ads only for now.

**Targeting:**
- Age: 18–26
- Location: Within 30km of target universities
- Interests: University, student life, travel, Ireland

**Ad formats:**
- Story ads with QR code — "Heading home for the weekend? Join the HTWA waiting list."
- Feed carousel — show 3 routes with price comparisons
- Reel ads — short video of a route journey

**Budget suggestion:** Even €5–10/day per university city will get meaningful reach. Start with Dublin and Galway, expand as the list grows.

## Waiting List Conversion Tips

1. **University-specific landing pages** (future) — "Join the HTWA waiting list for UCD" converts better than a generic form
2. **Referral mechanic** (future) — "Refer a friend, move up the list" drives organic sharing
3. **Progress updates** — email the waiting list monthly with build updates. Keep them warm.
4. **Early access positioning** — "First 100 signups from each university get founding member status" creates urgency
